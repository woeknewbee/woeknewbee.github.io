Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a sweater to my collection?

Navigate to the Collection tab and tap the "+" button in the top right corner. Fill in the details about your sweater including name, material, color, and condition rating, then tap Save.

How do I record a care session?

Go to the Care tab and tap the "+" button. Select the sweater you cared for, choose the care type (hand wash, machine wash, etc.), and add any notes about the process. You can also track condition changes.

Can I set care reminders?

Yes! Each sweater has a customizable care frequency (in days). The app tracks when your last care session was and shows upcoming care needs in the Overview section of the Care tab.

Where is my data stored?

All your data is stored locally on your device. Brenavexio does not use cloud services or internet connections. Your collection information, care records, and preferences are completely private.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings and tap "Clear Data". This will permanently remove all your sweaters, care records, and saved articles. Alternatively, uninstalling the app will also remove all data.

Can I recover deleted data?

Once data is deleted through the Clear Data function or app uninstallation, it cannot be recovered. We recommend being certain before clearing your data.

What materials does the app support?

Brenavexio supports tracking for Cashmere, Merino Wool, Alpaca, Cotton, Synthetic Blend, Mohair, Angora, and Silk Blend materials, each with tailored care instructions.

The app is running slowly. What should I do?

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, ensure your device has sufficient storage space and is running the latest supported iOS version. If problems continue, please contact us.

Getting Started

Quick Start Guide

1. Open the app and navigate to the Collection tab. 2. Add your first sweater with its details. 3. Go to the Care tab to log your first care session. 4. Explore the Stories tab for expert tips and guides. 5. Customize your experience in Settings.
