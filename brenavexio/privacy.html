Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Brenavexio is designed with privacy at its core. All your data is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

Information We Collect

Brenavexio does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. The application operates entirely offline and all data remains on your device.

  • We do not collect personal identification information
  • We do not collect usage analytics or behavioral data
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies
  • We do not collect device identifiers

Data Storage

All information you enter into Brenavexio (including your sweater collection details, care records, and preferences) is stored exclusively on your device using local storage mechanisms. This data is not accessible to us or any third parties.

Data Sharing

Since we do not collect any data, there is no data to share with third parties. Brenavexio does not integrate with any third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or data brokers.

Third-Party Services

Brenavexio does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data. The application functions entirely without an internet connection.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time through the Settings section of the application. Alternatively, uninstalling the application will permanently remove all stored data from your device.

Children's Privacy

Brenavexio does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age. The application is suitable for users of all ages as it does not collect any personal information.

Security

Your data is protected by your device's built-in security measures, including device encryption and biometric/passcode locks. Since data never leaves your device, there is no risk of server-side data breaches.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or the application's data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: support@brenavexio.app
