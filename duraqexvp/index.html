Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

ℹ
Privacy‑by‑design principle

Duraqexvp is designed with privacy at its core. All your maintenance records, cost data, schedules and preferences stay strictly local on your device and never leave it.

Information We Collect

Duraqexvp does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. The application operates entirely offline and all data remains on your device.

  • We do not collect personal identification information
  • We do not collect usage analytics or behavioral data
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies
  • We do not collect device identifiers

Data Storage

All information you enter into Duraqexvp (maintenance activities, cost records, schedules, knowledge‑base usage and preferences) is stored exclusively on your device using local storage mechanisms. This data is not accessible to us or any third parties.

Data Sharing

Since we do not collect any data, there is no data to share with third parties. Duraqexvp does not integrate with any third‑party analytics services, advertising networks, or data brokers. This is not a social network or shopping platform.

Third‑Party Services

Duraqexvp does not integrate with any third‑party services that collect user data. The application functions entirely without an internet connection.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time through the Settings section of the application. Alternatively, uninstalling the application will permanently remove all stored data from your device.

Children's Privacy

Duraqexvp does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age. The application is suitable for users of all ages as it does not collect any personal information.

Security

Your data is protected by your device's built‑in security measures, including device encryption and biometric/passcode locks. Since data never leaves your device, there is no risk of server‑side data breaches.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q1
    How do I record a maintenance activity? Open the Records section and tap the "+" button. Fill in equipment specifications, service type, cost breakdown, execution date, responsible technician, vendor, serial number, priority, completion status and facility location, then save your entry.
  • Q2
    How do I analyze maintenance costs? Open the Statistics tab and choose a 30‑day, 90‑day or all‑time view. Review total spend, average cost per activity, highest‑cost items and breakdowns by maintenance type to identify optimization opportunities.
  • Q3
    How do I schedule a future service activity? Open the Schedule tab and tap "+". Enter equipment name, service date, maintenance type, estimated duration, assigned technician and procedure notes, then enable a reminder for timely completion.
  • Q4
    Where is my data stored? All your maintenance records, cost analyses, schedules and preferences are stored locally on‑device. Duraqexvp does not use cloud services or internet connections. Everything stays private.
  • Q5
    How do I delete my data? Go to Settings and tap "Clear Data". This will permanently erase all records, schedules and preferences. Uninstalling the app will also remove all local data.
  • Q6
    Can I recover deleted data? Once data is cleared inside‑app or by uninstalling, records cannot be recovered. Use the export and backup options to keep a safe copy before clearing local storage.
  • Q7
    How do I use the technical knowledge base? Open the Knowledge tab and browse articles organized by technology processes, operation guidelines, troubleshooting, safety protocols and environmental standards for training and quick reference.
  • Q8
    The app is running slowly. What should I do? Close and reopen Duraqexvp. Verify device free storage space and update iOS to the latest supported release. Contact support if issues persist.
