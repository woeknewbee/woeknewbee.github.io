Mini speaker enthusiast logbook

NvenJMini Speaker Studio

Review these terms before using NvenJ.

I personally use quite a few mini speakers—some on shelves, others in drawers—but tracking repair records and details of speakers I’ve given away across scattered notes and chat logs was a hassle. I created "Mini Speaker Studio" to consolidate all this information into a single, offline logbook; you can simply open it and start recording—no accounts or cloud syncing required.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

ℹ
Privacy‑by‑design principle

NvenJ is designed with privacy at its core. All your speaker inventory, repair logs and gift‑hand‑off data stays strictly local on your device and never leaves it.

Information We Collect

NvenJ does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. The application operates entirely offline and all data remains on your device.

  • We do not collect personal identification information
  • We do not collect usage analytics or behavioral data
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies
  • We do not collect device identifiers

Data Storage

All information you enter into NvenJ (speaker inventory entries, repair tickets, gift records, tool calculations and preferences) is stored exclusively on your device using local storage mechanisms. This data is not accessible to us or any third parties.

Data Sharing

Since we do not collect any data, there is no data to share with third parties. NvenJ does not integrate with any third‑party analytics services, advertising networks, or data brokers. This is not a social network or shopping platform.

Third‑Party Services

NvenJ does not integrate with any third‑party services that collect user data. The application functions entirely without an internet connection.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time through the Settings section of the application. Alternatively, uninstalling the application will permanently remove all stored data from your device.

Children's Privacy

NvenJ does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age. The application is suitable for users of all ages as it does not collect any personal information.

Security

Your data is protected by your device's built‑in security measures, including device encryption and biometric/passcode locks. Since data never leaves your device, there is no risk of server‑side data breaches.

Changes to This Policy

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q1
    How do I add a new mini‑speaker? Navigate to the Speakers tab and tap the "+" button in the top‑right corner. Fill in model, driver size, battery specs, supported codecs, placement location and save your entry.
  • Q2
    How do I log a repair ticket? Open the Repairs tab and tap "+". Select the target speaker, fill out ticket number, fault description, consumed parts, labor hours and resolution state then save your repair log.
  • Q3
    How to record a speaker given as a gift? When editing or creating a speaker entry, fill in gift‑related fields: recipient name, packaging notes, tracking number and additional remarks for your personal reference.
  • Q4
    Where is my data stored? All your speaker inventory, repair tickets and gift records are stored locally on‑device. NvenJ does not use cloud services or internet connections. Everything stays private.
  • Q5
    How do I delete my data? Go to Settings and tap "Clear Data". This will permanently erase all speakers, repair logs, gift entries and tool preferences. Uninstalling the app will also remove all local data.
  • Q6
    Can I recover deleted data? Once data is cleared inside‑app or by uninstalling, records cannot be recovered. Confirm before wiping local storage.
  • Q7
    What are the built‑in helper tools? Battery life estimator helps you project runtime based on battery and usage parameters. Listening distance guide gives practical reference for speaker placement on shelves and desks.
  • Q8
    The app is running slowly. What should I do? Close and reopen NvenJ. Verify device free storage space and update iOS to the latest supported release. Contact support if issues persist.

Quick‑Start Guide

1. Open the app and navigate to the Speakers tab.

3. Navigate to Repairs tab to log repair history for units you have worked on.
4. Use built‑in battery estimator and listening distance guide for desk‑shelf arrangement.
5. View generated real‑data charts on Statistics tab.
6. Adjust application behaviour inside Settings.
